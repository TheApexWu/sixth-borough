# Cultural Assets — Bronx Hip-Hop 1973–1985

**Owner:** James Burke
**Purpose:** Track historical photo, video, and audio sourcing for the Bronx hip-hop cinematic. One row per asset, logged with source, license posture, and collection status. This is the working scratchpad — not a final citations doc.

---

## How to use this file

- **Status legend**
  - `[ ]` identified but not yet collected
  - `[~]` downloading / screen-recording in progress
  - `[x]` collected, in repo or local scratch
  - `[!]` license or sourcing problem — do not ship without resolving
- **Local path**: once collected, write the repo-relative path (e.g. `data/photos/bronx-hiphop/1520-sedgwick-01.jpg` or `data/footage/wild-style-clip-01.mp4`). Keep large files gitignored; note local-only paths with `(local only)`.
- **Reuse posture** — tag each asset:
  - `PD` public domain
  - `CC` Creative Commons (note which)
  - `FAIR-USE` transformative/educational reuse (acceptable as *source material* for the rendered pipeline; flag `[!]` before putting into the final submission video)
  - `PERM` have explicit permission
  - `UNK` unknown, needs research
- **Screen-record workflow**: for anything not directly downloadable (streaming docs, YouTube-only uploads), screen-record to `data/footage/raw/` at 1080p, then note the timestamp range used.

### Audio / music

Mostly for atmosphere in the pitch video, not the runtime. Keep this minimal — music licensing is the single biggest landmine.

- **Rapper's Delight** (Sugarhill Gang, 1979) — licensed, don't use raw
- **Planet Rock** (Bambaataa, 1982) — licensed
- **The Message** (Grandmaster Flash, 1982) — licensed
- **Kool Herc party tapes** — circulating, murky provenance, DO NOT assume clean
- **Alternative**: use Nemotron narration as the audio bed, skip music entirely, cite tracks on screen instead
